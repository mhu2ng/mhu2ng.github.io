I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Fudan University-SIMIS, advised by [Prof. Yinyu Ye](https://web.stanford.edu/~yyye/) and [Prof. Dongdong Ge](https://www.acem.sjtu.edu.cn/faculty/gedongdong.html).

Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering and my B.S. in Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research interests lie in operations research, optimization, and artificial intelligence. I am particularly interested in the interplay between mathematical optimization and machine learning, and I am currently exploring related topics under the guidance of my advisors.
